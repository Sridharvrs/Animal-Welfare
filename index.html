From the street to the sofa in five steps

Every rescue follows the same care path, and donors can follow along at each stage.

  1. Report

    A citizen calls our helpline or uses the portal to share a location and photo.

  2. Rescue

    The nearest ambulance reaches the animal, usually inside 45 minutes.

  3. Treat

    Vets stabilise, diagnose and treat with donor-funded medicine.

  4. Recover

    Rest, rehab and socialisation in a safe, loving shelter.

  5. Rehome

    Home-checked adopters give the animal a lifelong family.
